About MIFA

A student-led finance and investment association at MIT ADT University.

Who We Are

MIFA is a student-led finance and investment association at MIT ADT University. Its purpose is to create a platform where students from different academic backgrounds can develop practical knowledge of:

  • Financial markets
  • Corporate finance
  • Investments
  • Company analysis
  • Sector analysis
  • Valuation
  • Financial modelling
  • Investment research

Open to every student

Membership is open to students across MIT ADT University, not only finance or business students. If you are curious about markets, businesses and investing, MIFA is for you.

Mission and Vision

Mission

To develop practical financial and investment expertise among students through rigorous research, education, discussion, and real-world application.

Vision

To build a strong student finance community at MIT ADT University and eventually develop MIFA into a platform for research, collaboration, industry engagement, and inter-collegiate financial initiatives.

What Makes MIFA Different

Learn

Build strong foundations in finance and investing.

Analyse

Study companies, sectors, financial statements and valuations.

Apply

Use that knowledge through research, presentations, workshops and investment discussions.

From Education to Application

Each stage builds on the one before it: members learn the fundamentals, research businesses, analyse the numbers, discuss their conclusions and then apply what they have learned.
