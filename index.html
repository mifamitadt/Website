What We Do

A student-led platform for finance education, investment research, and market discussion.

Finance Education

Learn the fundamentals of financial markets, corporate finance, valuation, and investing through practical sessions and workshops.

Investment Research

Analyse companies, industries, financial statements, business models, and valuations.

Market Discussion

Discuss markets, sectors, macroeconomic developments, and investment ideas.

Practical Application

Turn concepts into research reports, stock pitches, valuation projects, and presentations.

Our Approach

MIFA encourages members to move beyond theoretical learning and develop practical analytical skills, by working through real businesses and real numbers.

  1. ResearchRead the annual reports, financial statements and industry material.
  2. AnalyseInterpret the numbers and test the business model.
  3. DiscussChallenge assumptions and hear opposing views.
  4. PresentCommunicate conclusions clearly in reports and pitches.
