Our Departments

A simple structure built around research, finance administration and communication.

Structure

MIFA's organisational structure is deliberately simple: the founder & investors, heads of departments, team members and general members.

The Three Departments

Investment Research & Analysis

The research core of MIFA. This team focuses on understanding businesses, markets, industries and investment opportunities.

Education is a function within this department, not a separate department.

Responsibilities

  • Company research
  • Sector research
  • Annual report analysis
  • Financial statement analysis
  • Valuation
  • Financial modelling
  • Investment theses
  • Stock pitches
  • Research presentations
  • Finance education sessions and workshops

Finance & Accounts

The team responsible for maintaining MIFA's financial records and managing its financial administration.

Responsibilities

  • Financial records
  • Income and expense tracking
  • Budgeting
  • Event-related finances
  • Financial documentation
  • Accounts management

Media & Communications

The public-facing team responsible for MIFA's communication, digital presence and visual identity.

Responsibilities

  • LinkedIn
  • Instagram
  • Research publication design
  • Event promotion
  • Announcements
  • Photography and event coverage
  • Recruitment communication
  • External communications

As MIFA grows, additional departments and specialized teams may be introduced.
