Research Process

  1. IdentifyChoose a company or sector worth studying.
  2. ResearchRead filings, annual reports and industry material.
  3. AnalyseExamine the financial statements and the business.
  4. ValueEstimate what the business may be worth.
  5. ChallengeTest assumptions and consider opposing views.
  6. PresentCommunicate the thesis clearly.

Research Philosophy

MIFA encourages evidence-based analysis rather than relying on market hype. Members are expected to understand the business, examine the numbers, question assumptions, consider opposing views, and communicate their conclusions clearly.

Disclaimer. MIFA's research is educational and student-led. It does not constitute financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell securities.
